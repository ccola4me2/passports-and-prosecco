Our story

Travel is personal. So is how we plan it.

Brent and Shannon have been friends for the better part of their lives, bound by an easy rapport and a shared love of seeing the world. Passports & Prosecco grew out of exactly that: two lifelong friends who decided to turn their passion for travel into something more, using it to secure the very best deals, perks, and experiences for the people they care about.

That is who we plan for, every single time: friends, family, and clients who deserve more than a booking engine. We founded Passports & Prosecco to give you something the websites can’t: real advisors who know you, advocate for you, and sweat the details so you don’t have to.

As independent advisors with Cruise Planners, we combine boutique, personal service with the buying power, protection, and perks of one of the largest travel networks in the country.
